Agents, the Bureau has recovered a prize. And it is gorgeous.

We have been sitting on this one for a while, and frankly it has been hard to keep quiet. The winner of Dominus Temporis will not go home with just a title and bragging rights. They go home with this.

>> 🏆 The Prize: A Hand-Engraved Playmat

Yes, that is a leather playmat, and yes, every line of that pattern is engraved by hand. Look closely: a Time Elemental, Saturn drifting past, a moon wearing its own clock face, and a banner at the bottom that says The Moose Is Out There — 2027.

It is one of a kind. There is only one, it is not for sale, and no, we will not make you a second one if you ask nicely. We checked. The moose was very firm on this.

Leather develops character over time, so this playmat will look better with every game played on it. Fitting for a prize about time, really.

>> ⏳ How Do I Get My Hands On It?

A quick refresher. Dominus Temporis is awarded at the Friday Main Event, and your score is simple:

DOMINUS SCORE

Game wins + Time Elementals in your main deck. Four copies means a four-win head start. Highest score at the end of the Main Event takes the mat. Tied? Most copies wins, then best final placement.

Which means the steps are, conveniently, three:

  1. Get a ticket and register for the Main Event. You cannot win a mat from your couch. We have checked that too.
  2. Start brewing. Time Elemental has to be in your main deck. Sideboarding it in does not count, however good the timing.
  3. Show up and win games. The more you win, the further ahead you are. Annoyingly, this part is on you.

Not planning on playing four Time Elementals? That is fine. Your opponents who are will thank you for the extra room at the top of the table.
