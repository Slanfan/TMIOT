>> 🗂️ Case File: ██████████

For months the Bureau has been quietly collecting fragments: notes tucked where nobody was supposed to look, and a pattern humming away beneath things we thought we knew by heart.

The evidence now points one way. ██████████ is not a rumor. ██████████ is real, and will most certainly be in the room with us. It could be someone you've traded with for years, or someone who has sat across the table from you more times than you can count. Suspicious? Very.

>> 🫎 vs 🕵️ Moose vs. Agents

The hunt for ██████████ is on!

This year everybody picks a side — well, nobody picks a side. Your check-in envelope contains a personal QR code. Scan it and you'll learn whether you're an Agent or a Moose. Keep that page handy. You'll want it.

Sharp-eyed investigators may already have spotted the signs. The rest of you will have to earn them. Happy hunting!

[ 🏆 The Faction Playoff ]

Good news for everyone who just missed the cut: this is a separate side-show. The main event runs exactly as usual, with Swiss and a Top 8.

The best Agents and Moose finishing just outside the Top 8 get a second shot at glory. The top 4 Agents and top 4 Moose each play their own Basic Land Bingo bracket, and the two bracket winners meet in a Grand Final: Agent vs. Moose.

The champion walks off with a one-of-a-kind █████████████ card, handed over on stage. Yes, with applause.

The moose has no gender. The moose simply knows.

>> 🇸🇪 New: Swedish-Legal Bonus

Play it pure, get +3. Simple as that.

For the Friday Main Event: register and play a deck that ticks both boxes below and you earn 3 bonus points.

100% Swedish legal

No reprints. Only cards from Alpha, Beta, Unlimited, Arabian Nights, Antiquities, Legends and The Dark.

Unpowered

No Black Lotus, Moxen, Ancestral Recall, Time Walk or Timetwister. Your wallet says thanks.

The bonus shows up in the standings only. It never touches the Swiss pairings, so nobody gets a cushier opponent. In plain words: consider yourself 3 points higher in the rank than your Swiss record says.

>> 🏆 Achievements: The Fine Print
Battle of the Clubs
Every point your club members score counts, across all formats and all rounds. The club with the highest average takes the trophy, so every single player helps.
⚠ You need to be in a club in Tolaria to count! Check your club status on Tolaria.app now. Club already exists? Ask your club administrator to add you. Can't find your club at all? Then it's time to start one: email us and we'll create it for you.
Dominus Temporis
Time Elemental must start in your main deck. Sideboarding it in doesn't count, no matter how good the timing. And the more you believe, the more you score: your Dominus score = game wins + Time Elementals in your main deck. Four copies means a head start of four wins, and you still have to go out and earn the rest. Highest score at the end of the Main Event takes the title. Tied? Most copies wins, then best final placement.
Urza Assembled
Get Urza's Mine, Urza's Tower and Urza's Power Plant onto the battlefield in the same game and then win that game. Assembling is the easy part, finishing the job is the real flex. A witness or a judge has to confirm it (no witness, no tron), and everyone who pulls it off walks home with their very own Urza Assembled diploma. Frame it. Brag about it.
Spice Lord
For the most creative, strangest deck of the weekend, judged by how many eyebrows go up. Tolaria will be set up so you can apply for Spice when you upload your deck photo at registration, and the spice committee will decide. Bring the heat!
Old School Bingo
Bingo is a Friday, Main Event thing. Your check-in envelope holds one bingo card with two 5×5 grids, so 50 tiny achievements in total. Cast a Black Lotus, win with Time Walk, watch a Chaos Orb flip miss … mark off each moment as it happens. Don't worry, there's something for everyone, and you'll tick off plenty without even trying. Every completed row earns you a prize, so hand them in as you go.
